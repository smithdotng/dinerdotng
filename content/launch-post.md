## So… what exactly is Diner.ng?

Diner.ng is on a mission to become **Nigeria's biggest directory of fun spots** — the restaurants, lounges, sitouts, cafés, hotels and event hotspots where life actually happens.

The goal is simple: make great places **easier to find, easier to love and easier to walk into.** No more "abeg, where una dey?" group chats. No more driving round the same roundabout four times because the address says *"opposite the big mango tree"*.

On Diner.ng you can:

- **Discover spots near you** — browse by city, by vibe, by craving. Suya at midnight? Brunch on Sunday? A quiet corner for a first date that won't end in a group photo? We've got you.
- **See the real menu before you go** — prices included. Yes, actual prices. Your account balance will thank us.
- **Scan, sit and order in peace** — every listed spot gets a QR menu. Point your phone camera, and the whole menu opens up. No app to download, no laminated plastic to wrestle.
- **Book a table** — at spots that take reservations, you can grab your table in seconds and walk in like a VIP. (Because you are one.)
- **Rate and review** — tell the world if the asun was fire or if it was *just* fire-adjacent. Your honest feedback helps spots serve you better.

## And for the restaurantpreneurs… this one is for you ❤️

If you run a restaurant, lounge, bar, café, hotel or event space, you already know: great food is only half the job. The other half is making every guest feel like they just walked into their favourite auntie's house — minus the questions about when you're getting married.

Diner.ng is your **launchpad**. Here's what's in the box:

- **A beautiful listing** that shows off your space, your photos, your opening hours and your story — so the right guests can find you.
- **A smart QR menu** you can update from your phone. Price of tomatoes went up again? Change it in seconds. Signature dish sold out? One tap and it shows *sold out*. No reprinting. No masking tape. We said what we said.
- **Guest ratings and reviews**, with the ability to reply — because a thoughtful reply to a tough review wins more hearts than any flyer ever will.
- **Table bookings and table management** on our Sweet plan, so you can see who's coming, seat them properly and keep things moving on a busy Friday night.
- **Featured placement** for Sweet spots, so hungry people find you first.

And because we like to start things on a sweet note, our **first customers get their first month from just ₦2,999**. That's less than a plate of small chops at some parties. Think about it.

From Abuja's quiet garden restaurants to Lagos rooftops that don't sleep, from Port Harcourt seafood joints to Enugu sitouts where every conversation turns into a debate — there is so much to celebrate.
